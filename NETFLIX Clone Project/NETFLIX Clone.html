Frequently Asked Questions

What is Netflix?
Netflix is a subscription-based streaming service that lets you watch a massive library of movies, TV shows, and original content on demand from almost any internet-connected device.
How much does Netflix cost?
In the US, Netflix subscription plans range from $8.99 to $26.99 per month, depending on whether you choose the ad-supported option, standard ad-free viewing, or the 4K Premium tier.
What can I watch on Netflix?
You can watch an enormous variety of content on Netflix, including blockbuster movies, hit TV series like Bridgerton and Stranger Things, award-winning documentaries, anime, reality competitions, and a massive selection of exclusive Netflix Originals.
Where can I watch?
You can watch Netflix on almost any internet-connected device with the Netflix app, including smart TVs, gaming consoles, smartphones, tablets, streaming media players, or directly through a web browser on your computer.
